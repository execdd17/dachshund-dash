Inputs: no activation; each is the raw number above. Hidden: tanh — each neuron adds up its weighted inputs and its bias, and tanh squashes that into −1…1. Outputs: linear — plain weighted sums of the hidden neurons (plus a bias); the highest score is the action it takes. positive negative: a neuron's value, or what a line is adding right now. How this works

This dog is played by a tiny neural network: 22 numbers in, 24 hidden neurons, 3 numbers out. Thirty times a second it reads the inputs above and picks the action with the highest score: nothing, jump, or duck (held).

It isn't told the rules or what anything is, and it never sees the picture: only the numbers above, read from the real game. The radar splits the screen ahead into four height bands, cut where the dog's own hitbox changes: something in the ground band would hit even a ducking dog, the head band only a standing one, and the two air bands only a dog in the air. In each band it's told when the next two things reach the dog and when they've gone past, in time rather than distance, so the right moment to act looks the same at any speed. A chocolate bar is just "something in the ground band"; a bird stack is three bands filled at once; a tower is all four.

How it learned: by evolution. A population of 150 random networks each played the same short runs; the best carried over and the rest were bred from the fitter half, with small random changes. Within minutes, networks that happened to jump at the right moment outscored the rest. Each tier adds what the game throws at it (0: chocolate and birds; 1: everything before the tower; 2: + the tower; 3: + the squirrel chase; 4: + the bosses and the frenzy), and a model named …-from-… started from the previous tier's best. One hit ends its run.

On the game: the faint colored layers ahead of the dog are the radar's four bands, in the same colors as the radar rows above. They run out to the ½ s ahead line, which moves out as the game speeds up: anything past it reads the same as an empty band, so the radar can't tell it's there yet. An empty band still says something ("nothing coming"), which matters as much as what's in one. The yellow boxes are what it's tracking inside that range (solid: the nearest), with tinted strips in each band they fill. Its current choice shows above the dog.
